Chosen vs learnedSettings are labelled Tuned (the backtest tried values) or Set by hand (picked, never tested). The prior curve and importance bars use the same grey for "preseason guess", so the two charts read together.
The overfitting counter follows you"7 variants tried" sits in the Lab header on every tab, is the hero of Versions, becomes "this run will be #8" in the Builder, and is an acknowledgement checkbox in Promote.
Compare gives a verdictv1.1 is better on every metric and proves nothing. A one-line verdict says so first; each delta card gives its noise range. v1.0 is grey circles, v1.1 blue squares, in every chart.
